I

Chapter I

Interface and Fuel

How you operate Osprey Core, and what powers it.

  1. Entry No. 01

    Crystal

    Always on your hotbar, the Crystal is your all-in-one interface for managing Vigil, your home, your mates, and more. Right-click to open it—there are no commands to learn in Osprey.

    See also Vigil No. 02 Jukebox Lookout No. 06

  2. Entry No. 02

    Vigil

    Vigil fuels Osprey. It replaces XP and is used to craft and maintain many of the world’s custom blocks and items.

    See also Crystal No. 01 Feather No. 11

II

Chapter II

Civilisation

Founding a civilisation and sharing ownership of it.

  1. Entry No. 03

    Standard

    When Osprey Core recognises that you’ve been playing alongside friends, it will prompt you to make a Standard. Place it in their company to establish a civilisation.

    See also Mate Shield No. 04

  2. Entry No. 04

    Mate Shield

    Craft a Mate Shield and drop it to a friend within your civilisation to promote them to Mate—effectively making them a co-owner.

    See also Standard No. 03 Warden No. 07

III

Chapter III

Awareness and Protection

Watching over your territory and reinforcing it.

  1. Entry No. 05

    Lookout

    Receive chat alerts about nearby players. Use a Lookout to stay informed about loitering and trespassing around your territory.

    See also Jukebox Lookout No. 06

  2. Entry No. 06

    Jukebox Lookout

    Log all nearby player activity and output directional redstone signals. Monitor potential loitering and access the activity log through your Crystal.

    See also Lookout No. 05 Crystal No. 01

  3. Entry No. 07

    Warden

    Reinforce a 16×16 area around this block. Toggle permissions for placing and breaking blocks, opening chests, using doors, and more. Your mates are exempt from these restrictions, and multiple Wardens can be stacked within the same area.

    See also Mate Shield No. 04

IV

Chapter IV

Transactions and Trade

Records of who took what, and trading without theft.

  1. Entry No. 08

    Ledger Chest

    A regular chest with a transaction history. Shift-click to see every transaction, who made it, and when.

    See also Ledger Book No. 09

  2. Entry No. 09

    Ledger Book

    Breaking a Ledger Chest drops a Ledger Book containing its full transaction history—a lasting record and irrefutable evidence of what took place.

    See also Ledger Chest No. 08

  3. Entry No. 10

    Shop Barrel

    Sell, restock, and price items through a dedicated shop interface, allowing safe trading without the risk of theft.

V

Chapter V

Banishment

What happens to the players you banish.

  1. Entry No. 11

    Feather

    Eliminate a player with the Banishment enchantment to claim their soul—their Feather. This physical item holds them in banishment in the Nether. Spend Vigil to extend their banishment time.

    See also Vigil No. 02

End of field guide
