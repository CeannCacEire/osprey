# Overview
Bonded is an armour enchantment that ties a piece of armour to the first player who wears it. From then on **only that player can wear it**. Anyone else can pick it up, carry it, store it, trade it or put it on an armour stand, but it won't stay on them.

It still drops when you die, like any armour. Your killer gets it but can't use it. All they can do is keep it from you, or sell it back.

| |  |
| :--- | :--- |
| Levels | Bonded I only |
| **Goes on** | Helmets, chestplates, leggings and boots (not elytra) |
| **Where it comes from** | Enchanting tables only, and rarely |

## Getting it
Bonded comes only from enchanting tables, on armour or on books, and only at level 15 or higher. It's rare: about a fifth as likely to come up as Protection, so it can take a lot of tries.

You won't find it from librarians, in loot chests, from fishing or on mobs' armour.
A Bonded book can be combined onto armour in an anvil as usual. The armour bonds to whoever wears it first after that.

## How the bond works
**Bonding**. The moment someone puts on a Bonded piece that has no owner yet, it bonds to them. 
They're told in chat, and the item gains the line "Bonded to [name]". Taking it off and putting it back on changes nothing.

**Someone else tries to wear it**. However they try (clicking or shift-clicking it into an armour slot, number keys, right-clicking with it, or on Bedrock), it comes straight back off into their inventory and they're told whose it is. If their inventory is full, it drops at their feet.

**Dispensers** won't put someone else's Bonded armour on a player, but they'll put it on its owner.

**Logging in wearing someone else's piece doesn't work either:** armour slots are checked when you join.
It never unbonds:
| Where | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Grindstone** | Refuses Bonded armour, so the enchantment can't be ground off. An unbonded Bonded book can still be ground. |
| **Anvil** | The result stays bonded. Renaming or repairing keeps the bond. |
| **Smithing table** | Upgrading to netherite keeps the bond. |

## Edge cases
• Stolen pieces spread the bond. If someone uses your bonded armour in an anvil, as repair material or to move its enchantments onto their own piece, the result is bonded to you. A thief can't launder your gear into theirs.

• The name doesn't update. "Bonded to [name]" shows the owner's name at the moment it bonded. If they change their Minecraft name later, the bond still holds; only the label is out of date.

• Whoever wears it first wins. An unbonded Bonded piece bonds to anyone who puts it on, including a thief who took it before you wore it. Wear new armour straight away.

• Armour stands are fine. Bonded armour can be displayed by anyone, since an armour stand isn't wearing it.

• Your killer keeps it if they want to. Bonded doesn't stop armour dropping; it only makes it useless to anyone but you. Expect ransom offers.
