# Overview

Banishment I–V is a weapon enchantment that can take a prisoner. When you kill a player with a Banishment weapon, there's a chance they're **banished**: sent to the Nether and kept there, while you get the **Feather of [Player]** that holds them.

It replaces Bane of Arthropods. Bane no longer appears anywhere on Osprey, and Banishment turns up everywhere Bane used to.

## Getting it

Banishment is an ordinary enchantment with the same costs and rarity Bane had. You can find it in all the places Bane used to come from:

• **Enchanting tables**, on a weapon.

• **Librarian trades**, as an enchanted book.

• **Loot chests**.

• **Mobs' gear**, on weapons they spawn with.

Books combine onto weapons in an anvil as usual. Enchanting and anvils spend Vigil from your XP bar.
If you pick up an old/glitched weapon or book with Bane of Arthropods, it turns into Banishment of the same level.

## How the roll works

Each level adds 20% to the chance of banishing:

| Level | Chance to banish |
| :--- | :--- |
| **I** | 20% |
| **II** | 40% |
| **III** | 60% |
| **IV** | 80% |
| **V** | 100% |

**The roll only happens when all of these are true**:

• **The victim is a player**. Mobs are never banished.

• **You land the killing blow in melee**, hitting them directly yourself. Arrows, tridents, lava, fall damage, TNT and pets don't count.

• **The Banishment weapon is in your main hand at that moment**. One in your off hand or inventory does nothing.

• **The victim isn't already banished**.

If the roll fails, it's a normal death. Either way, their items drop as usual and their Vigil bar is kept.

**Combat logging doesn't save anyone.** A player who logs out while combat-tagged leaves their body behind for a short time. Killing that body with a Banishment weapon rolls exactly like a real kill.

## What a successful roll does

• The victim is banished. They respawn somewhere safe in the Nether, within 1,000 blocks of its centre, and can't leave until they're freed. They skip their normal spawn point entirely.

• You get their Feather, the Feather of [Player]. If your inventory is full, it drops at your feet.

• The Feather starts with 24 hours of fuel. Feed it Vigil through your Crystal to keep them banished longer, up to 7 days at a time.

• They go free only when the fuel runs out. Destroying the Feather or killing its holder doesn't free them.

See the Feather for everything about holding, feeding, guarding and stealing Feathers.

# Life in exile
### Arriving
A banished player wakes up at a random safe spot in the Nether, on solid ground and away from lava, within 1,000 blocks of the Nether's 0, 0. The spot is picked while they're still on the death screen, so they never pass through their normal spawn and can't reach their own Feather on the way.

They arrive with nothing, since their items dropped where they died. Their Vigil bar is kept.

### What they can do
An Banished plays normally. They can mine, build, fight, trade and keep earning Vigil, and other players can still come and go through the Nether around them.

### What they can't do
| Way out | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Walking into a Nether portal** | Refused, with a message |
| **Riding through a portal in a boat, minecart or on a horse** | Refused |
| **Ender pearls, chorus fruit, or any other teleport to another world** | Refused |
| **Dying** | They respawn in the Nether. A respawn anchor they've set there still works. |
| **Logging out and back in** | If they're anywhere but the Nether, they're sent back |
| **Picking up their own Feather** | Refused. If it's somehow in their inventory when they log in, it drops.|

**Chat is limited too**. Exiles can't talk in Global. Their messages go to their Hearth's chat, and they can still read Global. An exile with no Hearth can't be heard by anyone until they're free.

### Finding their Feather
The Feather menu in an exile's Crystal shows where their Feather is: the exact coordinates and world, and whether it's carried by a player, sitting in a chest or lying on the ground. It never shows who holds it. While the menu is open, a carried Feather's position updates every few seconds. That location is what their Hearth needs for a rescue.

### Going free
The only way out is the Feather's fuel running out. After that, the next time the exile uses a portal, respawns, logs in or opens their Crystal, they're told "Your Feather has burned out. You're free to leave the Nether." and can leave like anyone else.

## Tips for the Banished
• Set a respawn anchor early. Then dying brings you back to your own base in the Nether.

• Check your Crystal often and pass the Feather's coordinates to your Hearth. A Feather on the move is being carried, and a carried Feather can be taken. Obviously this doesn't work if you don't have friends.

• Keep earning. Vigil earned in exile is yours when you get out.

• Remember it's a clock. Your captor has to keep paying, up to a week at a time. Many captors give up before you do.

• Set a respawn anchor early. Then dying brings you back to your own base in the Nether.
