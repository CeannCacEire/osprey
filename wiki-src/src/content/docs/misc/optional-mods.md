# Client Mods

Osprey is a vanilla-feeling server, and we want it to stay that way on the client side too.
The rule of thumb: **mods may improve your performance, your interface, or your comfort, so never any additional information.**

If a mod tells you something a vanilla player standing in the same spot could not know, it is banned.

Supported Java versions: **26.x and newer**. Bedrock players connect through Geyser and need nothing installed.

---

## Suggested mods

### Performance
| Mod | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sodium** / **Embeddium** | big fps |
| **Lithium** | more fps |
| **FerriteCore** / **ImmediatelyFast** | lower memory, faster rendering |
| **Iris** | shaders |

### Quality of life
| Mod | Why |
|---|---|
| **Mod Menu** | self explanitory |
| **AppleSkin** | shows vanilla hunger/saturation values, useful for incoming enchants |
| **Inventory Profiles Next** | sorting and inventory management |
| **Chat Heads** | Faces next to chat lines, very nice with global chat |
| **Simple Voice Chat** | Proximity voice, we have it installed on the server |
| **Xaero's Minimap / World Map** | **fair-play versions only** (see below) |
| **Litematica** | schematics for building. printer must stay off |


### Conditional
- **Xaero's minimap** — use the *Fair-Play* build, or disable entity/player radar and cave mapping. A minimap that shows mobs, players or ore is a banned mod wearing a hat.
- **Litematica** — schematics, overlays and material lists are fine. **Easy Place and the printer are not.**
- **Tweakeroo** — most tweaks are fine; freecam, player/entity highlighting and anything that automates clicking are not.

- **Flashback / ReplayMod** — as long as it's being used *only* for recording and fun purposes, using it to find things you haven't seen yourself ingame is explicitly wrong. We can see what you're using flashback for on our server, so don't even try. 
---

## Explicitly banned

Using any of these is a ban, not a warning. We know what mods you're using, and we have a custom anticheat specifically built to detect these things. We may not ban you immediately, and we may build a case on you to ensure we ban all involved parties. 

### Cheat clients
Wurst, Meteor, Impact, Future, LiquidBounce, Rusherhack, Aristois, KAMI, and any other "utility client", all banned outright, even with every module switched off. Having it installed is enough for us to ban you straight up, no questions asked bruh
### Information mods
- **X-ray / ore finders** of any kind, including x-ray resource packs and cave-culling tricks
- **Freecam / camera detach** — you don't get to look inside a vault you can't walk into
- **ESP, tracers, player/entity radar, chest or spawner finders**
- **Seed crackers** and seed-based ore/structure locators
- **Chunk-loading or chunk-caching mods** - the only ones allowed are Bobby, Voxy, and DH
- **Nether/overworld coordinate overlays that read unloaded chunks**

### Automation
- **Baritone** and any other pathfinding or auto-walk bot
- **Auto-clickers, macros and keybind scripts** that act on their own (combat, mining, farming, fishing)
- **AFK bypass** of any kind. Nothing on Osprey is meant to be farmed while you're asleep.
- **Litematica printer / Easy Place**, Schematica printer, or anything that places blocks for you

### Combat
- Killaura, aim assist, triggerbot, reach, velocity/knockback modification, auto-totem, auto-crystal, fastplace, criticals, hitboxes, anti-knockback, bhop, fly, speed, no-fall, timer, blink, and anything else in that family

### Accounts
- **Alt accounts are not allowed.** One account per player, full stop. Falcon has state of the art alt detection, we will not reveal how we find you, but our systems are effectively unbypassable even if you use a different computer, ip, house, mod list, settings - we will find you. 

- **VPNs and proxies** are checked at the proxy. If you need one for a legitimate reason, talk to staff first.

## Not sure?

If you have to ask whether a mod is allowed, that's usually a sign — but ask anyway. Ask staff before you use it, not after. "I didn't know" is not a defence once it's in your logs, and we'd much rather answer a question than process an appeal.
