## Custom code

**Falcon** — Osprey's own plugin, covering the Crystal, Hearths and background nation detection, Warden and reinforcement, Banishment and the feather, chat, grand gate portals, the Guide, custom enchantments, mob withdrawing, Vigil, and the anti-abuse and ban-wave tooling. Written for Osprey and not available anywhere else. Core systems are based off of - **[DefineOutside's work, engine is from Grim, and retrooper - under GPL3](https://grim.ac/)**

- **[Paper](https://papermc.io/)** — server core, and the anti-xray Osprey runs on
