# Overview
Vigil is Osprey's replacement for XP. You earn it by playing, it fills your normal XP bar, and it pays for everything XP buys in vanilla, such as enchanting and anvils. It also pays for most of Osprey's own blocks and upkeep.

There are no XP orbs on Osprey. Mobs, ores, furnaces, breeding, fishing, villager trades and bottles o' enchanting give no XP at all. Vigil is the only way to fill the bar.

**1 Vigil = 10 XP points**. Your bar holds up to **level 30**, which is **139 Vigil**.

## Earning Vigil
Vigil trickles into your bar as you play. Small actions earn fractions of a point that add up. Each kind of activity has its own hourly limit, so no single action can be farmed. Mixing activities earns the most.
The general rule is activity = vigil 

| Activity | Earns (XP points) | Hourly limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Being active** | 1 per minute | 60 |
| **Placing blocks** | 0.25 per block | 60 |
| **Mining blocks that take time to break** | 0.25 per block | 120, shared with ores |
| **Mining ores** | Coal 1; copper, iron, redstone, lapis 1.5; gold 2; diamond, emerald, ancient debris 5; nether quartz and nether gold 1 | Shared with mining |
| **Villager trades** | 2 per trade | 40 |
| **Killing hostile mobs** | 1 per kill; Wither 50, Warden 50, Ender Dragon 100 | 40 (bosses don't count toward it) |
| **Harvesting fully grown crops** | 0.25 per crop | 30 |

Remember 10 points make 1 Vigil. A normal mixed session fills the bar from empty in roughly 10 hours of play. Pushing every activity to its limit does it in about 4. Just play the game as you usually would, and you will have a constant stream of Vigil coming in. 

## What doesn't earn Vigil
• Standing still. You only earn the active bonus if you've looked around, clicked or done something within the last 2 minutes. Chatting doesn't count, and neither does being carried by water, a minecart or an AFK pool.

• Placing and breaking the same block. Breaking a block you placed in the last hour earns nothing, and neither does placing a block where you broke one in the last hour.

• Spawner mobs, and kills the game doesn't credit to you.

• Killing players.

• Creative and spectator mode.

• Relogging. It doesn't reset your hourly limits.

## The Vigil bar and Vigil items

Vigil comes in two forms, and you can switch between them freely:

| | In your XP bar | As Vigil items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What it is** | Your normal XP bar | A glinting echo shard named "Vigil", worth 10 XP |
| **Limit** | Level 30 (139 Vigil) | None: stack and store as much as you like |
| **When you die** | Kept | Dropped like any other item |
| **Can be traded, stored or stolen** | No | Yes |

When your bar is full you stop earning. Spend some, or withdraw it as items, to make room.

## Withdrawing and depositing
Open your Crystal and click the Vigil icon. It shows how much Vigil you hold, how much room is left before the cap, and what you've earned in the last hour.

![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569189646737342524/9hsfw0k.png?ex=6af28d3d&is=6af13bbd&hm=420871de7e0557cb36738efa5485ce00f27003ed28852a1bff91697c9a0cfd2c&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

• **Withdraw 1, 10 or All turns bar Vigil into Vigil items**. If they won't fit in your inventory, nothing is taken. "All" withdraws as many as fit.

• **Deposit** turns every Vigil item in your inventory back into bar Vigil, up to level 30. Anything over the cap stays as items.

Converting never creates or destroys Vigil: 1 item is always exactly 10 XP points.

Vigil items can't be crafted into vanilla items. Since they look like echo shards, they won't make recovery compasses.

## What Vigil is used for

This list is updated to 11/8/26, subject to change.

**Everything XP buys in vanilla**. Enchanting tables and anvils work exactly as normal and spend from your bar.

**Crafting Osprey's blocks and items**. These recipes take Vigil items:

| Item | Recipe |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Standard** | A named banner + 8 Vigil |
| **Warden** | A lodestone + 8 Vigil |
| **Greater Warden** | A Warden + 4 diamond blocks + 4 Vigil |
| **Jukebox Lookout** | A jukebox + 8 Vigil |
| **Noteblock Lookout** | A note block + 4 Vigil |
| **Ledger Chest** | A chest + 4 Vigil |
| **Shop Barrel** | A barrel + 4 Vigil |
| **Corroded Copper** | A copper block + 4 Vigil |

**Fuel, upkeep and repairs**. These take Vigil straight from your bar, through each block's menu:

| What | Cost |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Feather** | 1 Vigil adds 1 hour of banishment, up to 7 days |
| **Warden** | Loses 5% of its health a day. 1 Vigil restores 50 health (about 1 Vigil a day); a Greater Warden takes about 25 a day |
| **Nether portal** | Each trip takes 4 Vigil from the portal's tank, which holds 40 and drains 4 a day |
| **Shop Barrel repairs** | 1 Vigil restores 25 health |

## Death, theft and Edge Cases

• **Dying keeps your bar**. Only your items drop, so Vigil in the bar is safe from raiders.

• **Vigil items drop on death** and can be stolen like anything else. Keep large savings in the bar, or somewhere well defended.

• **Mending doesn't exist on Osprey**. Librarians never offer it, loot and fishing never give it, and anvils won't carry it over. Old Mending items do nothing, since there are no XP orbs to repair with.
