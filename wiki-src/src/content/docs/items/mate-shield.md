# Overview
The Mate Shield is how a Captain makes someone a Mate. It's a real shield carrying your Hearth's banner, and it's the only way to promote a member to Mate.

A Hearth has three ranks:

| Rank | Who |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Captain** | One per Hearth: the founder, or whoever the Captaincy was handed to. |
| **Mate** | The Captain's officers, up to a limit set by the Hearth's tier. |
| **Crew** | Every other member. |

The shield is a symbol of the rank. The rank itself is recorded by the server, so a Mate who loses their shield is still a Mate.

## Making one
Only the Captain can make a Mate Shield.

1. Put a plain shield in your inventory.

2. Open your Crystal and click Roster.

3. Click Make a Mate Shield. The plain shield turns into a Mate Shield carrying your Hearth's banner.
It costs nothing beyond the shield.

## Handing it over
Drop the shield in front of the member you want to promote. When a Crew member of your Hearth picks it up, it binds to them and they become a Mate. They're told straight away.

• Whoever picks it up first gets it. Any Crew member of your Hearth can pick up an unbound shield, so drop it where only the right person can reach it.

• Outsiders can't pick it up at all.

• The Captain and existing Mates can carry it without anything happening, so a Mate can deliver it for you.

• Your tier sets the limit. Once your Hearth has as many Mates as its tier allows, a Crew member can't take another Mate Shield.

| Tier | Accepted members | Mates allowed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camp** | 3+ | 1 |
| **Village** | 6+ | 2 |
| **Town** | 12+ | 3 |
| **City** | 24+ | 4 |

## What a Mate can Do
| Action | Crew | Mate | Captain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Open the Roster and see suggested members | No | Yes | Yes |
| Accept suggested members | No | Yes | Yes |
| Expel Crew | No | Yes | Yes |
| Expel a Mate | No | No | Yes |
| Place, take down and run Wardens | No | Yes | Yes |
| Break the placed Standard | No | Yes | Yes |
| Make and unmake Mates | No | No | Yes |
| Hand over the Captaincy | No | No | Yes |

Basically, warden switches can be flipped by Captain and Mates. Crew can see them but not change them. 

**Becoming Captain**. The Captain can hand the Captaincy to a Mate from that Mate's page in the Roster. If the Captain hasn't played for 14 days, the Mate who has held the rank longest becomes Captain automatically. If you have some dispute about Captaincy, make a ticket in our Discord, and staff will handle it for you. Please be respectful when dealing with staff bruh

## Losing the Rank
| What happens | The Mate | The shield |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The Captain clicks Make Crew again on their Roster page | Back to Crew | Becomes an ordinary shield, banner kept |
| They leave the Hearth or are expelled | No longer a member | Becomes an ordinary shield, banner kept |
| The shield is lost or destroyed | Still a Mate | Gone. The Captain can make a new one. |

Only the Captain can expel a Mate, other mates can't.
Please note: for right now there is no confirmation for transferring ownership, so don't fucking spam click the menus, the change is irreversible unless your mate is feeling kind. 

## The shield Item
• **It blocks like any shield**. Carry it into a fight if you like, features or buffs may be added onto Mate shields in the future, stay tuned

• **Only its Mate can pick it up once it's bound**. Nobody else can take it, even if it's dropped.

• **It can be stored in chests** like any shield.

• **It can't be enchanted or repaired**. Anvils and enchanting tables refuse it.
