# Overview
A Shop Barrel lets you sell to other players while you're offline. It looks like an ordinary barrel. You stock it with one kind of item and set up to five prices, such as "32 Bread for 1 Diamond". Anyone else who opens it sees the trades and clicks to buy. Their payment waits inside the shop until you collect it.

## Making and placing one
**Recipe**: a barrel in the centre of the crafting grid, with 4 Vigil above, below and on both sides of it (the corners stay empty). 

![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569176547745726656/z8f5r1j.png?ex=6af2810a&is=6af12f8a&hm=b64c72f2c6681695a813bc5a511b5ee03cc762b27df4fc8e98ebdb6948aaa115&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Naming**: rename the barrel in an anvil before you craft it. The shop's menu shows that name. An unnamed shop is called "[Hearth]'s Shop", or "[player]'s Shop" for a Wanderer.

![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569176783478194227/zqz0b1p.png?ex=6af28143&is=6af12fc3&hm=d9bb97f63539c25146bb6a6d110ee8228a352ec1b93593a8c5daf0c11679801a&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Placing**: put it down anywhere you're allowed to place blocks.

**Who owns it:**

• **Placed by a Hearth member**, it belongs to the whole Hearth. Any member can stock it, set trades, collect payments and break it.

• **Placed by a Wanderer**, it's theirs alone, and it becomes their Hearth's when they join one.

• If its Hearth dissolves, it goes back to the player who placed it, and to their new Hearth if they're in one.

## Setting up trades
Right-click your shop to open the owner's view. The top five rows show the current page, and the bottom row holds the controls:

![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569177176677294080/tv5jx54.png?ex=6af281a0&is=6af13020&hm=029ad6d64edd68259da3179edddee99ec2842f09cb745d373a6070037e91953f&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

This shop menu shows 58 Obsidian in inventory, in the Stock menu.

| Control | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Stock** | The goods for sale. Put them in and take them out like a chest. |
| **Proceeds** | Every payment lands here. |
| **Trades** | Opens the trade editor. |
| **Preview** | Shows what buyers see, without letting you buy. |
| **Info** | The shop's name, owner, stock, how many trades the stock covers, and its health. |
| **Health** | Shows its health and repairs it (see Breaking and protection). |
| **Close** | Closes the menu. |

## The trade editor
The editor has five rows, one per trade: the item for sale, an arrow, the price, and a Remove button.

1. Set the item for sale. Pick up the item and click the first slot with it on your cursor. The slot copies it, with as many as you're holding. Your item stays yours.

2. Set the price. Do the same in the price slot, for example with a diamond.

3. Fine-tune amounts. With an empty cursor, left-click a set slot to add 1 or right-click to take 1 away. Amounts stay between 1 and a full stack. To set a smaller amount quickly, right-click a stack to pick up half before clicking the slot.

A trade shows to buyers once both sides are set. Remove clears that row.

## Rules for trades
• One kind of item per shop. All five trades sell the exact same item, each at its own price, such as "32 Bread for 1 Diamond" and "8 Bread for 2 Iron Ingot". To sell something else, take the stock out first.

• A trade can't ask for the item it sells. Bread for bread is refused.

• Feathers and the Crystal can't be sold or asked for. Vigil and other custom items can.

## Stocking it
Open Stock and put the goods in. Only items identical to the item for sale go in: same name, enchantments, damage and contents. A renamed loaf won't stock a bread shop. Nothing goes in until you've set up a trade.

## Buying from a shop
Right-click someone else's Shop Barrel. Each row is one trade: the item for sale, an arrow, and the price.
Hover over the item for sale to see the stock, such as "In stock: 128 Bread, enough for 4 more trades".

![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569178419076538408/bmpbt51.png?ex=6af282c9&is=6af13149&hm=6de6b4c4577e226a72c507dfeff1bf9f44f82aaead0e3bef283dd59d6a89a239&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Click either item in a row to buy that trade once.** The price leaves your inventory and the goods arrive. If something's wrong, nothing changes and you're told why:

| Message | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Out of stock** | The shop has run out of goods. |
| **You need 1 Diamond** | You don't have the price on you. It can be anywhere in your inventory, but it must be identical: a renamed diamond doesn't count. |
| **This shop's proceeds are full** | The owners need to collect their payments before it can sell again. |
| **Make room in your inventory** | You need space for the goods. |

You can buy from a shop inside another Hearth's Warden field, even if that Warden locks its chests. Owners can't buy from their own shop.

## Stock and earnings
• **Each page holds 45 slots.** Stock and Proceeds each have their own 45.

• **Collect your payments**. When Proceeds is full, sales stop until an owner empties it.

• **Hoppers can't touch it**. Hoppers, droppers and hopper minecarts can't feed stock in or pull payments out, so neither you nor a thief can automate it.

**• There's no sales log or alert**. Watch the Stock and Proceeds pages, or the Info icon, to see how it's doing.

## Breaking and protection
A Shop Barrel has 500 health. Its owners break it instantly, but anyone else has to wear it down first.
| What hits it | Health lost |
| :--- | :--- |
| **An outsider mining it** | 1 per second each. Clicking faster doesn't help. |
| **An explosion** | 25 each |

A raider sees the shop's health on screen as they work, such as "Shop: 470/500". One raider takes a full shop down in about 8 minutes, five in under 2, or about 20 TNT explosions.

When it breaks, by an owner or at 0 health, its stock and payments spill on the ground and the Shop Barrel drops, keeping its name. 

• **It never drains on its own**. An untouched shop costs nothing to keep.

• **Repair it with the Health icon in the owner's view**. Left-click repairs 25 health for 1 Vigil, right-click repairs fully (20 Vigil from empty). Vigil comes from your own Vigil bar.

• **A Warden protects it**. Inside a Warden's field with breaking switched on, the Warden takes the damage instead, until its field is down.

• **Fire burns it like a normal barrel**, spilling its goods where it stood.
