# Overview
A Ledger Chest looks and works like an ordinary chest, but it remembers every item put in or taken out, by whom and when. Its owners can read that record at any time and they're alerted when an outsider takes something. Use them to covertly log resources, check on Vaults, and secure detailed transactional information about your possessions. 

To anyone outside your Hearth it's just a chest, nothing about it gives it away. 

## Making and placing one
**Recipe**: a chest in the centre of the crafting grid, with 4 Vigil above, below and on both sides of it (the corners stay empty).
![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1568827671658631269/xhxn3rt.png?ex=6af13c20&is=6aefeaa0&hm=9ddc9c5cabeb25d561091758e66da6ce0ef17e9c9b71ecc246c74a1a0db4936e&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Naming**: rename the chest in an anvil before you craft it, for example "Storage". The name shows in its log, its alerts and the Ledger Book it leaves behind. An unnamed one is called "a Ledger Chest".
![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1568829956686086194/7jwpxzz.png?ex=6af13e41&is=6aefecc1&hm=4c1ef1601288b3b6036387d505207bd49ba5a9ba85fb5036deec17b41dee8e51&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Placing**: put it down anywhere you're allowed to place blocks.

**Who owns it**: it belongs to your Hearth, and every member can read its log and receives its alerts. If you're a Wanderer it's yours alone, and it becomes your Hearth's when you join one.

**Opening it**: anyone who can open a chest there can open this one, exactly as normal. If it's inside a Warden's field, the Warden's settings decide who may open containers.

## What it records
It records every item put in or taken out, by everyone, members and outsiders alike.

• **Players**: when someone closes the chest, it compares what was inside when they opened it with what's inside now, and records the difference for each kind of item, such as "Took 12 Iron Ingot" or "Put in 5 Oak Log".

• **Two players at once**: if more than one person had it open at the same moment, the entry names them all ("MRDOGO5779or Steve"), since the chest can't tell whose hand moved what.

• **Hoppers, droppers and hopper minecarts**: recorded too, as "A hopper", and added up per item for each hour, so even a busy hopper line only adds a few lines a day. This is how you'll spot a raider draining it from below.

Opening the chest and looking without changing anything isn't recorded. Entries are kept for 30 days.

## Reading the log
Sneak and right-click the chest with an empty hand. On Bedrock, sneak and tap it with an empty hand. An ordinary right-click opens the chest as usual, and sneak-clicking while holding something places it against the chest as normal.

For anyone outside your Hearth, sneak-clicking just opens the chest, so it can't be spotted that way.
The log opens as a chest menu, newest first, 45 entries to a page:

• Each icon is the item itself, stacked to the amount moved, and named "Took 12 Iron Ingot" or "Put in 5 Oak Log". Hover over it to see the player, the time and the chest's position.

• The filter icon cycles between everything, taken out and put in.

• Clicking an entry shows only that player's entries. Click the head icon to show everyone again.

• Arrows move between pages.

Bedrock players get the same log as a form. You can also open a log straight from one of its alerts (below). Unlike Lookouts, Ledger Chests have no list in the Crystal.


## Alerts
Members are alerted in two cases. Taking items out and breaking the chest only alert you when an outsider does it.


| Alert | When |
| :--- | :--- |
| Taken from | An outsider takes items out. Every online member gets a chat line each time, such as "tybug2 took 12 Iron Ingot from Storage". |
| Broken | Anyone outside its owners breaks it. |

Both also go to the Crystal's Alerts inbox, kept for a week. There, the same player at the same chest within 10 minutes is grouped into one line ("3 times"); the log still has every item. Clicking an alert opens that chest's log, if the chest is still there.

Ledger alerts aren't sent to Discord.

## Breaking one
When a Ledger Chest breaks, its contents spill like any chest's, its log is deleted, and whoever broke it gets a Ledger Book holding its latest 100 entries. What else they get depends on who they are:

| Who breaks it | They get |
| :--- | :--- |
| A member, or the Wanderer who owns it | The Ledger Chest back, still named, plus the Ledger Book. |
| An outsider | A plain chest (the Vigil is lost) and the Ledger Book. They're told whose it was, and the owners get a "broke your Ledger Chest" alert. |

Pistons can't move it and explosions don't destroy it. See the Ledger Book page for how the Book works.
