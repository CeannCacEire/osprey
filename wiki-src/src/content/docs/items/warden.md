# Overview

A Warden is the block that protects your Hearth's land. It projects a square field around itself, from bedrock to the sky. Inside it, outsiders are stopped from building, breaking, opening chests and the other things its switches cover. Your Hearth's members always act freely.
There's no reinforcement on individual blocks. The Warden itself has health instead. Every time an outsider tries something it blocks, it loses a little. At 0 its field falls until a member feeds it Vigil again.

## Crafting and placing one

**Recipes**
**Warden**: a lodestone in the centre of the crafting grid with 8 Vigil around it.
![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569197529927524412/b0z8ht4.png?ex=6af29495&is=6af14315&hm=d540069553f80cad595f9cb6c48e89bf3da0dadbd3e2db0e629afcdf47ebb9b7&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Greater Warden**: a Warden in the centre with 4 diamond blocks and 4 Vigil around it. A plain lodestone won't do; it has to be a Warden.
![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569198118141034496/7t1v2rr.png?ex=6af29521&is=6af143a1&hm=d704403c5f45a2f81a2a568d699be98a7c99f9dc06dca176c60a01e191622464&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

**Placing**. Only your Hearth's **Captain** or a **Mate** can place one, and only on your Hearth's own land. Its field can't overlap another Hearth's Warden field. If it's refused, you're told why. There's no limit on how many you can place, but Wardens close together cost more to keep up (see Crowding).

A new Warden starts at full health with every protection switched on and the field outline off.

**Taking it down**. Only your Captain or Mates can break a Warden, whatever its health. It drops as its item and keeps its health and switches, shown in its description ("Health: 950/1000"), so moving it never resets anything.

**Pistons and explosions can't move or destroy it**. If your **Hearth** dissolves, its Wardens stop protecting and anyone can break them.

## The field and its switches
The field is a square centred on the Warden, covering every height from **bedrock** to the **sky**.

**Opening a Warden's page**

Right-click the Warden (any member), or open your Crystal and click the **Warden** icon to list all your Hearth's Wardens. The page shows its health, how long that lasts at the current upkeep, its seven switches, and the feeding buttons. Any member can see the switches, but only the Captain and Mates can flip them.

![](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1384087503522496593/1569199235369410642/8vqk4h0.png?ex=6af2962c&is=6af144ac&hm=fa6a6186762bfef75ba5a98fc5f4f8055b150e2fbb2c2944b16bdccf1d6443b1&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)


| Switch | Stops outsiders from |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Placing blocks** | Placing any block, emptying water or lava buckets, hanging item frames and paintings, placing armour stands |
| **Breaking blocks** | Breaking any block, filling a bucket from a block, breaking item frames, paintings and armour stands. Also stops explosions set off by outsiders. |
| **Doors, trapdoors, gates** | Opening or closing them |
| **Buttons, levers and the like** | Using buttons, levers, pressure plates, tripwires, note blocks, bells and daylight sensors, and changing repeaters and comparators |
| **Chests and containers** | Opening chests, barrels, shulker boxes, furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces, hoppers, droppers, dispensers, brewing stands, crafters, lecterns, chiseled bookshelves, decorated pots, jukeboxes, and chest minecarts and boats. Ender chests are never blocked. |
| **Animals and crops** | Hurting, killing, leashing, shearing, milking, dyeing or breeding animals, and trampling farmland. Harvesting crops counts as breaking blocks. |
| **Show the field** | When on, particles outline the field's edge at eye height for anyone within 48 blocks |

Turn a switch off to let outsiders do that freely, such as opening a gate for visitors, without costing the Warden anything. Hostile mobs are never protected.

## Health, upkeep and feeding
A Warden loses 5% of its full health every day, whether or not anyone attacks it. Any member of your Hearth, Crew included, can feed it from their own Vigil bar using Feed 1, Feed 10 or Feed until full on its page.

| | Warden | Greater Warden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loses each day** | 50 health | 250 health |
| **1 Vigil restores** | 50 health | 10 health |
| **Upkeep alone** | 1 Vigil a day | 25 Vigil a day |

**At 0 health the field is down** and outsiders can do anything there. The Warden itself still can't be broken by them. Feeding it any amount brings the field straight back.

## Crowding
You can place as many Wardens as you like, but each one within 16 blocks of another of your Hearth's Wardens decays faster:

| Other Wardens within 16 blocks | Upkeep multiplier | Daily upkeep per Warden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **None** | ×1 | 1 Vigil |
| **1** | ×1.5 | 1.5 Vigil |
| **2** | ×2.25 | 2.25 Vigil |
| **3** | ×3.4 | 3.4 Vigil |

The daily figures are for ordinary Wardens; Greater Wardens scale the same way from 25. Each Warden's page shows how crowded it is and what it costs per day.
Why stack them? Where fields overlap, an outsider has to wear down every one of them in turn, starting with the healthiest. Stacking is how a Hearth puts more health behind its most important ground.

They scale exponentially, so you could theoretically make an invincible base as long as you can front the cost, for example in times of war; you could stack hundreds of wardens on the castle walls to make them impregnable. 

## Raiding a Warden
Anything an outsider tries that a switch blocks is stopped, and costs the Warden 1 health. The outsider sees why on screen, along with the Warden's health, such as "`Protected by MRDOGO5779's Warden (870/1,000)`", so a siege can watch itself working.

• **Spam doesn't help**. Each outsider costs a given Warden at most 1 health per second, however fast they click.

• **Instant blocks cost nothing**. Breaking grass, flowers, torches or crops is blocked but doesn't wear the Warden down.

• **Explosions set off by outsiders break nothing inside the field**, but cost 1 health for each block they would have broken. Your own members' TNT works normally.

• **Overlapping fields of the same Hearth**: each try costs whichever Warden has the most health.

| Attackers | Full Warden | Full Greater Warden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 raider** | About 17 minutes | About 83 minutes |
| **5 raiders** | About 3½ minutes | About 17 minutes |

Defenders can keep feeding it Vigil the whole time.

## Corroded Copper
Raiders can also bring Corroded Copper, a copper block crafted with 4 Vigil in a plus shape. A Warden's field never stops it being placed, and pistons can push it in from outside.

• **It eats Warden health on its own**: 300 health over 5 minutes, about 1 a second, while the raiders keep mining. It doesn't count toward any raider's once-a-second limit.

• **It targets** the enemy Warden whose field covers it, choosing the healthiest if several do. It never attacks its placer's own Wardens.

• **You can see it working**. It oxidizes from fresh copper through exposed and weathered to oxidized as it's used up. When spent, it becomes a plain oxidized copper block.

• **Stacking it is weak**. Within 16 blocks, each extra copper does a third of the one before: 300, then 100, then 33, and so on.

• **It pauses** if its field goes down or it's pushed out, and carries on when it's back in a field.

• **Defenders can mine it out** at normal copper speed. Inside a field with breaking switched on, only the Warden's own Hearth can break it, so raiders can't take it back. It drops with its remaining corrosion and can be placed again.

• It doesn't stack, so a raider carries one per inventory slot. Wax, axes and weathering don't change it.

## Alerts
• **Under attack**: your Hearth's online members are told when a Warden is attacked, at most once every 5 minutes per Warden. Corroded Copper starting on a Warden raises the same alert and names whoever placed it. Further alerts follow as the Warden's health runs low and when its field goes down.

• **The anvil Alert**: every time attacks take another 100 health from a Warden, whether from raiders, explosions or Corroded Copper, every online member hears an anvil land, wherever they are.

• **Alerts also go to your Crystal's Alerts inbox**, and to Discord if your Hearth has set it up.

## Wanderers: personal Wardens
Players without a Hearth can protect their things too, at a price.

• **Ordinary Wardens only**. A Wanderer can't place a Greater Warden.

• **Anywhere** except where its field would overlap someone else's field.

• **Only you are let in**. Only you can feed it, flip its switches and break it. Your Crystal's Warden icon lists your own Wardens.

• **Three times the upkeep**: 3 Vigil a day for one on its own.

• **Strict crowding**: each of your Wardens within 16 blocks multiplies upkeep by 4. Two together cost 12 Vigil a day each, and three cost 48 each.

• **Joining a Hearth hands them over**. When you found or join a Hearth, your Wardens become the Hearth's, at normal Hearth upkeep from then on, and its members are let in.

## Edge cases and how to use Wardens well

These are recommendations by the guy who wrote the code for Wardens:
 
**Edge cases worth knowing**

• **It's a column, not a bubble**. The field runs from the bottom of the world to the top, so tunnelling under your base doesn't get a raider past it. But it's a square: the corners sit about 22 blocks from the Warden, and the middle of each edge only 16. Measure from the edge, not the corners.

• **A dead field isn't a lost Warden**. At 0 health outsiders can do anything inside, but they still can't break the Warden. The danger is the window while it's down, not the block.

• **Corroded Copper ignores the placing switch**, and pistons can shove it in from just outside the edge. Valuables near the edge are the easiest to reach.

• **Switches you turn off cost nothin**g, for you or for outsiders. An open gate is truly open.

• **Fields 17 to 32 blocks apart overlap without crowding**, because crowding only looks 16 blocks out. This works today, but treat it as a loophole that may be closed as soon as i finish writing this fucking wiki

• **If your Hearth dissolves**, every Warden stops at once. Leadership changes don't matter, but disbanding does. Maybe consider feathering the Captain?

**Recommended usage**
1. _Vault in the middle_. Put the Warden at the heart of what you're protecting, and keep your best chests well inside the edge.

2. _One Warden per thing that matters_. Use a Greater Warden for a whole town, and ordinary Wardens layered over the vault. Stacking is for the vault, not the farmland.

3. _Keep a Vigil reserve by the Warden_. A lone raider needs about 17 minutes to empty a full Warden. If someone's online to feed it, a small raid never gets through.

4. _Listen for the anvil_. Every clang is another 100 health gone. Two clangs in a row means feed it now, not after the fight.

5. _Pair it with Lookouts and Ledger Chests_. The Warden slows raiders down, a Lookout at the edge tells you they've arrived, and a Ledger Chest tells you what they got.

6. _Show the field once, then turn it off_. It's great for laying out your base, but it also shows every raider within 48 blocks exactly where your protection ends.

7. **Wanderers**: protect the chest, not the crib. At three times the upkeep, cover what you can't lose, and join a Hearth when you can.
