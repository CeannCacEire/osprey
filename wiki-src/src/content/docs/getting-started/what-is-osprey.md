## a vision of the future

speaking candidly as one of the anonymous developers of the server, osprey is a culmination of everything we love about minecraft. it's a love letter to the genre of survival and what makes minecraft, minecraft. the idea was born when we thought to ourselves: 

what do we love about minecraft? 

we love the freedom to explore a world, the freedom to make our decisions, to discover mechanics, to be creative, and a player guided community. osprey at its core without the fancy tech and the simplicity of it all is all about the players and the community. osprey has been around since 2020, and for the better part of a decade has been the home of thousands of players. 

however, minecraft is changing as a title, and servers are no longer as simple as they are before. servers are plagued with slop, microtransactions, pay to win mechanics, endless admin overreach, and immature players who annoy and harass people just trying to have a good time. 

if you have a problem, you have the freedom to solve it. don't like what another player says? mute them yourself. you've been robbed? end them yourself and banish them to hell for their sins!

as a member of the team that runs osprey in the background, we promise to never overreach or use our power unless it is absolutely necessary, and we believe in never taking action unless the community is unable to come to a peaceful out of game consensus. 

we created osprey to make a dream server for ourselves to play, and to hopefully play with likeminded players. we hope osprey becomes a home for you as well.


# overview
Osprey is a Vanilla+ Minecraft Survival Server based upon a 5000 × 5000 island that aims to solve the issues plaguing many other servers. As a player-led server, griefing, looting, aggressive admin oversight and excessive commands are all addressed. No claims, no resets, no wands, no rollbacks, no scripts, no commands. And yet, thanks to Falcon, our proprietary backend software, you can build, trade, fight and dominate without a second thought. All systems are built directly upon vanilla, making Osprey fully cross-compatible with Bedrock clients without any compromise.

Gather your friends, build a kingdom, and reign supreme on Osprey.
