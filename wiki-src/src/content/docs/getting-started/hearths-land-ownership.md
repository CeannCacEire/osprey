# Overview
A Hearth is a group of players who live together and have chosen to make it official by raising a Standard. Members share a name, a banner, ranks and land, and together they can protect that land and watch over it.

There's no overt claiming on Osprey. (fuck towny and factions) Nobody draws borders or types a command. Falcon works out on its own who lives where and with whom, just by watching how people play. Your land is simply the ground you and your Hearth have actually shaped.

## How Falcon sees where you live
Every few minutes the server looks at what everyone has been doing and works out three things.

**1. Where you live.** It notices where you build, store things in chests and set your bed. The spot where you've done the most becomes your home. Recent activity counts most, and old activity slowly fades.

**2. Who you live with.** It notices when two players do things together, and it only counts acts that go both ways:

| Builds trust | Breaks trust |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Building near each other at the same time** | Breaking blocks the other placed |
| **Both putting things into the same chest** | Breaking their bed |
| **Handing each other items** | Hurting them |
| **Setting beds close together** | Taking from a chest they use without ever putting anything in |

Being nearby, visiting, walking through someone's town or buying from their shop earns nothing either way. Trust grows slowly and drops fast.

**3. Settlements.** Players who trust each other and live close by are grouped into a settlement. A player who lives alone is a homestead.

**Living there counts**. Someone who makes their home and sets their bed inside your settlement's land can join it even without trust, unless they've been hostile to you. Spies are part of the game, which is why being in a settlement doesn't make anyone a Hearth member, your **Captains** still decide that.

You can always see what Falcon thinks with the Here icon in your Crystal: "Home" on your own land, another Hearth's banner and name on theirs, or "The Wilds" anywhere else.

## Founding a Hearth
Once Falcon sees you living with at least 2 others, you get the advancement `A Place Worth Keeping` and the Standard's recipe unlocks.

1. Design a banner in a loom and name it in an anvil. The name becomes your Hearth's name.

2. Craft it with 8 Vigil around it to make a Standard.

3. Place it on your settlement's land with 2 other members of your settlement within 16 blocks, online, and not in a Hearth. They're your witnesses.

The whole server hears that your Hearth has been founded. You become its Captain, your witnesses become Crew, and everyone else in your settlement shows up as a suggested member.
Solo players can't found a Hearth. See The Standard for the full details.

## Members, suggestions and expelling
**Falcon suggests**, your leaders decide. Anyone the server groups with your Hearth's settlement who isn't a member yet, isn't in another Hearth and hasn't been expelled from yours shows up as a suggestion. Nobody can apply to join and nobody can be added by name so a player has to actually live with you first.

Your **Captain** and **Mates** manage members from the **Roster** in the Crystal:

| Action | Who | What happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Accept** | Captain or Mate | The suggested player becomes Crew and is told. |
| **Expel** | Captain (anyone but themselves) or Mate (Crew only) | They're removed, told, and never suggested to your Hearth again. |
| **Lift an expulsion** | Captain or Mate | Click them in the Roster's Expelled list, and they can be suggested again. |
**Membership sticks.** Once you're accepted moving away doesn't remove you. You stay a member until you leave or are expelled.

## Ranks and tiers (subject to change)
| Rank | Who | Can |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Captain** | One per Hearth: the founder, or whoever the Captaincy was handed to | Everything a Mate can, plus make and unmake Mates, expel Mates, and hand over the Captaincy |
| **Mate** | Promoted by the Captain with a Mate Shield | Accept and expel Crew, see suggestions, place and run Wardens, break the placed Standard |
| **Crew** | Every other member | Everything membership gives (see below) |

The only way to make a Mate is the Mate Shield. See its own page.
Tiers grow with your number of accepted members, and set how many Mates you can have:


| Tier | Members | Mates allowed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camp** | 3+ | 1 |
| **Village** | 6+ | 2 |
| **Town** | 12+ | 3 |
| **City** | 24+ | 4 |

A Hearth that shrinks drops back a tier. For now, tiers only change your Hearth's title and its Mate limit.

## Land

Your Hearth's land is the ground its **accepted member**s have actually shaped: where you build, store things and sleep. The server divides the world into squares 32 blocks across. A square becomes yours once your members have done enough there.

• **It grows as you build and shrinks as old activity fades**. Land nobody uses gradually stops being anyone's.

• **Borders form where two Hearths meet**. If both have been active in the same square, the busier one owns it. If they're close, the square is contested.

• **Land is updated every few minutes**, in the same pass that groups settlements.

• **Suggested members don't count** toward your land until they're accepted.

Land alone doesn't protect anything. It's where you can raise your Standard and place Wardens. The Wardens are what stop outsiders.

## Seeing it

• **In game:** the Crystal's Here icon tells you whose land you're standing on, live.

• **On the web map**: each Hearth's land is drawn as an outline in its banner's colour, showing its name and tier. The outline is deliberately blocky and approximate, and updates every few minutes. The map shows the surface only, has no player positions, and redraws its terrain about once an hour.

## Leaving, succession and dissolving
**Leaving**. Any member can leave from the Hearth menu in their Crystal, with a confirmation click. A Captain has to hand over the Captaincy first, unless they're the last member.

**Handing over**. The Captain can make any Mate the new Captain from that Mate's page in the Roster. The old Captain becomes a Mate, and the whole Hearth is told.

**Succession**. If the Captain hasn't played for 14 days, the Mate who has held that rank longest becomes Captain automatically. With no Mates, it goes to the longest-standing Crew member.

**Dissolving**. When the last member leaves, the Hearth dissolves. The whole server is told, and the name is free for someone else. Its Wardens stop protecting and anyone can break them, and its Shop Barrels go back to the players who placed them.

## Why Should I have friends and found a Hearth?
|  | Hearth member | Wanderer (no Hearth) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wardens** | Your Hearth's Wardens let you in. The Captain and Mates place them, any member can feed them, and upkeep is normal. | Personal Wardens only: small, three times the upkeep, strict crowding |
| **Lookouts and Ledger Chests** | Shared: every member gets the alerts and can read the logs | Yours alone |
| **Shop Barrels** | Any member can stock them, set prices and collect payments | Yours alone |
| **Alerts** | Every member is told about attacks, intruders and theft, in game and on Discord | Only your own |
| **Hearth chat** | Yes | No |
| **On the web map** | Your land, name and tier | Nothing |

When a Wanderer joins a Hearth, their Wardens, Lookouts, Ledger Chests and Shop Barrels become the Hearth's.
