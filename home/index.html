what is osprey?

osprey is a CivSMP, a new server meant to solve all issues with modern slop servers. it's a specific type of server aimed at a very specific type of player. the player that is sick of the same four genres, and wants the best of all worlds with none of the drawbacks.

as a player-led server, greifing, looting, annoying admin oversight, and excessive commands are all completely solved. no stupid towny claim system, in game balance or p2w currency, no wands, no rollbacks, no decade old plugins, and no commands to remember.

and yet thanks to falcon, our proprietary backend, you can build, trade, fight, and build community without a second thought. all systems are directly integrated into vanilla minecraft, just as notch intended. this also makes osprey fully cross compatible with bedrock clients of any generation.

sick of having to build a base 10,000 blocks away because you're worried someone is going to take your shit? tired of every single server being the same copy paste hermitcraft clone? osprey has a solution to all of these problems. we can confidently state, "we have solved modern minecraft".

if you want to see what it's about- gather your friends, play minecraft your way, and reign supreme on osprey!

Falcon

Falcon is the software that runs Osprey. It logs every game packet to track player activity and detect cheating. It also powers custom items and enchantments, connects the server to Discord, and gives staff a web panel to monitor the server and keep lag down.

For now, how Falcon works stays private. We may open-source it one day.
